No eyes, no shoulders, no chest, no hands, no objects. The face fills frame, mouth centered horizontally and near vertical center, mouth spans about 70 percent of image width. Aim for a polished, credible luxury skincare magazine photograph, never a dental clinical before-after result and never a traditional portrait. Generate at high resolution, ideally 2048x2048 or larger, square photo suited to a later rectangular crop. No text, watermarks, jewelry, props, frames, gradients, overlays, colored fog, teal/turquoise/coral casts, computer-rendered look, excessively perfect gums, duplicated or fused teeth.
